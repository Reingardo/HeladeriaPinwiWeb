## Prompts finales

### Pinwi

Use case: background-extraction. Asset: photoreal transparent product cutout for a Pinwi frozen treats website. Reference image is a pile of real lime-green Mexican bolis in clear plastic bags. Isolate ONE representative boli and show it upright vertically, full product visible. Match the real long slim flexible tube of lime frozen ice, tiny ice crystals, wet glossy transparent cellophane and triangular heat-sealed ends from the reference. It is a simple bag of frozen lime ice, no wooden stick, no rigid cardboard, no bottle, no label or invented branding. Maintain natural irregularities and realistic volume. Soft neutral studio light, no cast shadow and no surface. Genuine transparent alpha background. Single object centered with modest padding; no text, no collage, no extra objects.

### Bollo

Use case: background-extraction. Asset: photoreal transparent product cutout for Helados Pinwi Pinwi. Reference photo shows frozen Mexican gourmet bollos Gansito. Extract and present ONE of these actual bollo products, upright vertically, complete top and bottom visible. Preserve the real short plump cylinder of pale strawberry-cream frozen dessert with cake/chocolate/red filling pieces, tightly wrapped in thin crinkly transparent plastic, rounded packed ends and a small colorful Gansito label matching the reference. It is a sealed cylindrical bag dessert, not a bottle, not a jar, no tied pointed neck, no stick. Preserve photographic food and plastic texture. Remove all surrounding products, cardboard advertising boxes, background and tabletop. Genuine transparent alpha background. Center one single object with modest padding, soft neutral studio lighting, no cast shadow, no added wording or invented claims.

### Nieve

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: this exact Oreo ice cream cup photograph. Change only the background: remove the blue-and-pink backdrop and surface to genuine transparent alpha. Preserve the real transparent thin disposable plastic cup, the flat packed Oreo cookies-and-cream ice cream surface, cookie crumbs and whole Oreo cookie on top, and the long bright blue plastic spoon, its angle and shape. Keep the actual product proportions, photographic texture and viewing angle. Full cup and complete spoon visible with modest padding, centered, no cut-off edges. No redesign, no spherical scoop on top, no paper cup, no new logo, no text, no other objects, no cast shadow.
